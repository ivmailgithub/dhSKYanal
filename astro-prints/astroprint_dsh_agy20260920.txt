  ### Root Causes of the "Solid Squares" Bug & Engineering Fixes

  #### 1. Why Constellation Names Printed as Solid Squares

  1. Tiny Font Sizing & No Tracking: The previous script drew text at only 2.4 mm – 3.6 mm cap height with default
  font tracking (0.2 mm inter-letter gap). On a 3D print, adjacent perimeters from neighboring letters fused together.
  2. Coarse Cell Resolution: The previous grid was only 360 × 180 cells. For a 130 mm (or 180 mm) cylinder, each cell
  was over 1.13 mm × 1.11 mm—wider than the entire spacing between letters!
  3. Over-Aggressive Max-Pooling: If any single pixel inside an 11 × 11 pixel block touched a letter stroke, the
  entire 1.13 mm × 1.11 mm cell was converted into text filament (Class 1).
  4. 5-Pass Diagonal Hole-Filling: The script ran a 5-pass diagonal contact dilation loop that filled in every inner
  loop (erasing the holes of O, A, R, B, D) and bridged adjacent letters into solid rectangular blobs.

  ### Key Upgrades Implemented

  #### 1. Expanded Geometry for 180 mm Diameter × 250 mm Height
  • Outer Diameter: 180.0 mm (Radius R = 90.00 mm). Fits standard 256 × 256 mm build plates.
  • Cylinder Height: 250.0 mm (fits within 256 mm Z-travel).
  • Circumference: C = π × 180.0 ≈ 565.49 mm.
  • Wall Thickness: 2.40 mm (Inner Radius R_{inner} = 87.60 mm). All 5 colors cut completely through the full wall.
  • Surface Area: +73% more space than the 130 × 200 mm tube, providing ample breathing room for lettering, star
  fields, and calendar scales.

  #### 2. High-Resolution Grid Matching 0.4 mm Nozzle
  • 3MF Grid: 1440 circumferential × 625 axial cells.
      • Circumferential resolution: 565.49 mm/1440 = 𝟎.𝟑𝟗𝟑 mm/cell
      • Axial resolution: 250.0 mm/625 = 𝟎.𝟒𝟎𝟎 mm/cell
      • Every cell matches the physical line width of a 0.4 mm nozzle!
  • Calibrated Line & Disc Widths:
      • Constellation Lines: 0.60 mm width (2 solid perimeters on 0.4 mm nozzle).
      • Stars: Magnitude 0 to 5 discs scaled from 3.6 mm down to 0.80 mm (≥ 2 extrusion perimeters; zero dropouts).
      • Coordinate Grid: 0.40 mm grid lines, 0.80 mm Celestial Equator.

  #### 3. Crisp, Tracked Typography & Collision Avoidance
  • Typography:
      • Prominent Constellations (ORION, URSA MAJOR, CASSIOPEIA, etc.): 6.5 mm cap height, 1.3 mm character tracking.
      • Secondary Constellations: 4.8 mm cap height, 1.0 mm character tracking.
  • Open Counters: The aggressive 5-pass diagonal hole-filling was eliminated for text. The centers of O, R, A, B, D
  remain open.
  • Collision-Free Layout: Fine-tuned offsets for dense regions (CORONA BOREALIS, DELPHINUS, LEPUS, LUPUS, SERPENS)
  eliminate overlapping bounding boxes across all 70 visible constellations.
  #### 4. Cylinder Projection Orientation
  • Top Rim (Z = 232 to 250 mm): Calendar Month Strip (DECEMBER through JANUARY) with 10-day decan ticks.
  • Bottom Rim (Z = 0 to 18 mm): Right Ascension Hour Scale (23h down to 0h with 1-hr, 30-min, and 10-min ticks) and
  Star Magnitude Scale.
  • Sky Projection: All constellation names read horizontally around the circumference when the dew shield is
  standing vertically.

  ### Slicing Recommendations for 0.4 mm Nozzle

  1. Open dewshield_h2c_5color.3mf in Bambu Studio / OrcaSlicer.
  2. Select 0.4 mm Nozzle and 0.16 mm Optimal or 0.12 mm High Quality layer height.
  3. Under Quality → Wall Generator, choose Arachne (captures fine letter strokes and sharp star edges).
  4. Set Infill to 100% Aligned Rectilinear (avoids air gaps within the colored wall).
  5. Add an outer Brim (5–8 mm) to stabilize the 250 mm tall cylinder on the build plate.
